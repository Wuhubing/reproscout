149                                However, Hagger et al.’s depletion manipulation skips the initial key step of forming a habit and there is no impulse to override when complex rules are introduced (Baumeister & Vohs, 2016a; but see Hagger & Chatzisarantis, 2016, for a commentary). Thus, it is possible their procedure is not sufficiently depleting. To test the robustness of the ego depletion effect, researchers need to employ effective depleting tasks (Baumeister and Vohs, 2016a, Dang et al., 2017). Taken together, more adequately powered, pre-registered future studies are needed to draw strong conclusions about the ego depletion effect in general and the depletion effect on unethical behavior in particular.
