257:Recent systematic reviews of neurofeedback with functional magnetic resonance imaging (fMRI-nf) [@tursic2020] and neurofeedback with functional near infrared spectroscopy (fNIRS-nf) [@kohl2020] miscalculate the statistical power and statistical sensitivity of several studies they review. The fMRI-nf review overestimates the mean and median statistical power of included studies by about `r difMRIPower %>% round(0)` times and the statistical sensitivity by about `r difMRISensitivity %>% round(0)` times (see Table 1 for recalculated values and comparisons). The fNIRS-nf review, on which I (RTT) was a coauthor, overestimates power by about `r difNIRSPower %>% round(0)` times and sensitivity by about `r round(difNIRSSensitivity/.5)*.5` times (see Table 2).
