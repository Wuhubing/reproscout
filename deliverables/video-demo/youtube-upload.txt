Title
ReproScout — Scientific Reproduction Review | MIT 1.125 | Weibing Wang

Description
ReproScout is my independent project for MIT 1.125.

I developed a workflow for importing prepared scientific projects, running original code, inspecting execution evidence, recording reviewer decisions, and exporting portable reports.

Repository: https://github.com/Wuhubing/reproscout
Public evidence website: https://wuhubing.github.io/reproscout/

This demonstration uses the authenticated application on a development host. The public website is currently a read-only evidence archive. Recorded acceptance results are clearly labeled; saved reviews are attributed to an AI assistant.

Narration uses an authorized AI clone of my own voice.
